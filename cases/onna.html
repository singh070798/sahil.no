Onna grew salad and vegetables using vertical farming, in closed hydroponic growth houses located close to the biggest cities. All choices and effort to minimize the environmental footprint of agriculture. At Komité, our team rebranded the company, and I made the investor film and its soundscape as part of that work.

The problem

Onna had a strong idea but struggled to get investors on board. Growing food indoors near the cities it feeds is simple to say but hard to picture, and the company needed a way to make that vision feel concrete and credible. A voiceover wasn't possible at the time, so the film had to tell the whole story in text alone, and the pacing had to give viewers enough time to read every frame without letting any of them drag.

The approach

The client trusted us to write the script, so we shaped the content ourselves. We treated the film as a series of headlines and talking points, the highlights of the pitch, each landing in a bold typeface. The lead designer on the rebrand provided rough storyboards, and I took them from there, handling the animation, timing, edit and soundscape. The tone had to feel green and natural, but also modern and disruptive. Fast-paced, percussion-heavy music and a quick run of varied scenes gave the film that energy, while the timing of each headline kept it readable.

Result

After the rebrand and the launch of the investor film, Onna raised investment well above what it had asked for. Seeing the work play a part in that is what I'm proudest of on this project. And we also ended up with a lot of fun logo vignettes from the exploration phase which I can now show off.
