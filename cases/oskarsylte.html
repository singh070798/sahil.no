Oskar Sylte is one of Norway's oldest soda producers, based in Molde. They have been a long-running retainer client at SDG\TBWA, and I've made social media content for them over several years. This case focuses on 2025–2026, when I delivered a steady stream of posts.

The challenge

The budget was limited, but the goal was always to deliver the best possible work within it. Motion performs better than stills on social media, so I pushed for animated posts wherever possible. The recurring ask was to present the products in a variety of ways that where simply not feasible to create in real life. At the very least not with the limited budget and time constraints often presented.

The approach

First, I built 3D models of all of Oskar Sylte's products, so we were no longer limited to existing pack shots. That allowed us to free ourselves from the constraints of the current asset library. Now we could present the products from any angle and in any scene we wanted. Whether it be inserting the products into stock footage or create entire 3D scenes from scratch. Or at times create entirely abstract scenes that had no intentions of looking real.

Result

We managed to create a steady flow of high quality animated posts at a level the budget wouldn't otherwise allow. Oskar Sylte's dedicated audience has received the content well, without the negative feedback CGI at times can get. I believe that's because the core brand was respected with the delivery of these visuals. They weren't created just because motion and 3D were trendy, but to elevate the brand.
