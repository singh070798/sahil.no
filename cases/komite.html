Komité is a design agency I joined as a motion designer in 2020. One of my first tasks was to make the agency's showreel, built from a set of projects the team had chosen to represent who they are.

The challenge

I was new, and I didn't know any of the projects beforehand. I had to get to know each one well enough to present it with confidence. That turned out to be a benefit. Working through the material was a crash course in the agency itself, what we deliver, what we do well and what we could do better. The bigger challenge was standing out. Most agency showreels look alike. Fast cuts, loud music, as much work as possible in as little time as possible.

The approach

I went the other way and slowed things down. Instead of overwhelming the viewer, the reel gives each project room to be seen. Rather than jump-cutting for the sake of efficiency, I built a transition between every scene, so one project flows into the next. The result plays as one smooth, continuous experience rather than a highlight montage.

Result

The team was closely involved throughout and very happy with the result. The reel became Komité's go-to introduction, used on the website, on social media, in pitches and in client meetings. It gave the team a quick way to show the breadth of the work without walking through individual case studies, and it helped win both clients and pitches. For me, it was also a way to show what I could do as a new member of the team.
