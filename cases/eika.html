Eika is an alliance of independent local banks across Norway. This project was a series of animated campaign assets for Eika's credit card, made for social media and email. I worked on it as a motion designer and 3D artist at Komité, brought in by Folka, who had Eika as a client.

The challenge

Eika already had a 2D animation style for their brand that they were happy with. What they needed was content in the same style introducing the benefits of their new credit card. This included visualizations for social media and email campaigns. For which formats and designs could be changed and updated on short notice. Making each new variation frame by frame would have been slow, and hard to keep consistent. Pushing us into the realm of 3D.

The approach

I recreated their existing 2D style in 3D in Blender, using 2D toon shading with outlines so the renders read as flat illustration. The goal was for the new content to feel like it came straight from the original animation, while the underlying setup was built for reuse. With the scenes, objects and look in place, new variations became a matter of adjusting and re-rendering rather than starting over. Matching a flat, illustrated look closely in 3D was the hardest and most satisfying part of the job.

Result

A set of social media and email assets in Eika's established style. The 3D setup paid off a year later, when Eika came back for more content and we could build on it straight away.
