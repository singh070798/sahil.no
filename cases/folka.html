Folka is a communication agency specialising in pedagogy. Endringsledelse (change management) is an animated explainer that works as one of their mini courses. I made it through the design agency Komité, handling both the animation and the sound design.

The challenge

Change management is an abstract subject with a lot of moving parts. Folka supplied a voiceover, a brand guide and a rough storyboard. My job was to turn that into moving images that made the ideas easy to follow, without losing the course's substance.

The approach

I took my cue from the title sequence of The Morning Show, where a handful of coloured dots carry an entire story without a single word. The film uses the same idea: Simple circles with become the main characters, and using Folka's colours stand in for people, groups, ideas and stages in the model. The same shapes regroup into a question mark, a phone, a clock and a target, so one scene flows into the next rather than cutting away. Most of the work went into those transitions, so the viewer can follow the thinking as one continuous line. I built the soundscape alongside the animation so every movement has its own small sound.

Result

A finished explainer film that now forms part of Folka's mini course on change management. The client was very happy with the result.
