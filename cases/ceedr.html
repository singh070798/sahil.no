Ceedr is a growth-focused digital marketing agency, previously known as The Engine. SDG TBWA handled the rebrand, and I was the motion designer on the team, responsible for the brand film and a set of animated brand elements.

The problem

The old name and identity no longer reflected how Ceedr works: data-driven, digital and built around technology. The new brand needed to say that clearly, starting with a brand film to introduce the name. Ceedr also communicates mostly through presentations, reports and its website, so the identity needed motion elements that could live in those everyday formats, not just in a launch video.

The approach

The brand film was storyboarded by the rebrand's design lead. I handled the execution and sound design, turning the boards into a finished film. We chose an AI voiceover, partly because of a tight schedule, but also because being an early adopter of new technology fits the brand itself.

For the brand elements, we went in the opposite direction from the precise, analytical nature of Ceedr's work. A small set of abstract shapes, each with its own surface and texture, moves slowly and softly. They bring warmth and feeling into presentations and reports, capturing the emotion behind the work in a different medium than the numbers themselves.

Result

A brand film to launch the new name, plus a library of animated brand elements that Ceedr uses across its presentations, reports and website.
