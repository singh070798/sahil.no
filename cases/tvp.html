The Viking Planet — Posters

Role
Motion Design
Year
2024
Tools
Illustrator, After Effects
Tags
Campaign, Digital Out-of-Home

The Viking Planet is a digital Viking museum in the centre of Oslo. For their summer campaign to attract tourists, SDG TBWA created a series of posters that tie Viking-age phrases and activities to modern ones. A cruise becomes a Viking raid, and "going to Hel" simply means visiting the exhibition. I animated the posters for digital surfaces online and for out-of-home screens around Oslo city centre.

The challenge

The brief was to animate only the text and leave the visuals alone. The museum wanted to keep its digital content a surprise for visitors, so the posters shouldn't give anything away or set up false expectations. But static images with moving type risk being overlooked on a busy street, and the posters needed to catch the eye.

The approach

I suggested adding minimal motion to the imagery itself, just enough to draw attention to each poster's subject. Viking content often invites something loud and epic, and we deliberately went the other way. Each still image was given an elegant 2.5D treatment that brings it slightly to life with depth and slow movement, without revealing anything. And setting up emotional expectations rather than visual ones. Using AI to create the effect was also considered, but we skipped it because it gave too little control and cost too much in quality. Doing it by hand kept every frame true to the original photography.

Result

A successful campaign that ran across digital channels and on out-of-home screens in Oslo city centre, drawing attention on the street while keeping the museum experience a surprise. The client was happy with the work and went on to reuse the 2.5D elements in later productions.
