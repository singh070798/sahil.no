Silent Eight — Brand Elements

Role
Motion & 3D
Year
2022
Tools
Blender, After Effects
Tags
Motion Identity, 3D

Silent Eight builds AI that helps banks detect and fight financial crime, such as money laundering and sanctions breaches. TBWA Helsinki, who had Silent Eight as a client, brought in the TBWA office in Norway to create a set of animated brand elements. I worked on them at SDG TBWA as motion designer and 3D artist.

The challenge

The brief started as a very abstract idea: iridescent liquid loops. There was no storyboard or fixed form, just a feeling and a material. My job was to turn that into motion that worked as brand elements, flexible enough to live across Silent Eight's surfaces, from the website to other channels.

The approach

I built the loops in Blender as fluid, glass-like ribbons that constantly twist and fold into themselves. There are two versions: one traces an infinity shape, the other a simple circle. Both are endless forms that suit a loop with no beginning or end. The iridescent surface shifts colour as it turns, giving an otherwise dark, serious subject some life and light. On top of the 3D, I added small tracking callouts in After Effects: circles that lock onto points on the surface, labelled with case IDs and "Person of interest." They hint at what Silent Eight actually does, finding the few suspicious signals within a huge flow of data, without making the visual literal. Turning such an open, abstract brief into something concrete is the part of this project I'm proudest of.

Result

Two seamless looping brand elements, the infinity and the circle seen from various angles, used across Silent Eight's surfaces, including their website.
