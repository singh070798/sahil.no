CST — Brand Elements

Role
Motion Design
Year
2024
Tools
Photoshop, After Effects
Tags
Motion Identity, 2D animation

Canadian Seafood Treasures (CST) was founded by groundfish harvesters in Atlantic Canada to build long-term value, stability and global awareness for sustainably harvested Canadian groundfish. They commissioned their brand film from a studio specialising in hand-drawn animation. At SDG\TBWA, I was tasked with creating a set of animated brand elements that emulate that style, mainly for CST's website.

The challenge

The elements had to sit right next to the hand-drawn film without looking like something else. The illustrations for the brand had already been commissioned and finished. The challenge was to recreate the look and feel of hand-drawn animation applied to already finished illustrations that weren't designed or rigged with this task in mind.

The approach

We already had beautiful illustrations of CST's fish and a few commissioned scenes. I prepared them in Photoshop and rigged them for animation in After Effects. Then I emulated the imperfections that make hand-drawn animation feel alive. Texture shifts from frame to frame to mimic brush strokes, and Posterize Time lowers the frame rate so the motion steps the way drawn animation does. Small added elements and effects complete the effect, so the rigged illustrations carry the feel of the hand-drawn film.

Result

A set of animated brand elements that sit seamlessly alongside the hand-drawn brand film, used mainly across CST's website.
