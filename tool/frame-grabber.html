Frame grabber

Open a video, step to the exact frame you want, and save it as a full-resolution still. Handy for posters, thumbnails and preview images.

Input
A video: MP4, WebM, MOV
Output
A PNG or JPG of one frame
Runs
In your browser. Nothing is uploaded
