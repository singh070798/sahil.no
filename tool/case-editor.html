cases/case-untitled.html Preview
How this works. Fill in the fields below. The preview on the right updates live and stays in view while you scroll the form. Colors, type and layout match the real site; the animated logo is simplified to a plain wordmark here.
  1. Fill in this form for one project.
  2. Under Export, below the preview, download the case page into your cases/ folder.
  3. Copy the manifest entry next to it into cases/manifest.json. The Homepage card tab previews how that card looks.
  4. To edit a page later, use Load a case page… above and pick the file — everything reloads into the form.

Export

Each case needs both: the page itself, and an entry in the manifest that puts it on the homepage. Both update as you edit the form.

Step 1

The case page

Save it in your cases/ folder.

Step 2

The manifest entry

Add it to the list in cases/manifest.json.

Where you place it in the list is where it appears on the homepage and in the Prev/Next order. Add a comma before or after it to match its new neighbors.
