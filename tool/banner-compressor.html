Tools

Banner compressor

Drop in HTML5 banner zips. Photos are re-encoded, file references in the HTML and CSS are updated to match, and each banner is rezipped as small as it needs to be to get under your limit.

Input
HTML5 banner zips
Output
Compressed zips, one per banner
Runs
In your browser. Nothing is uploaded

Settings

1 KB is counted as 1,000 bytes, the stricter reading of most ad specs. Quality starts high and only steps down until the banner fits. Images are only shrunk once the lowest quality isn't enough, 10% at a time.
